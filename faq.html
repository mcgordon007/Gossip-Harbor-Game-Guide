FAQ

Get answers to the most common questions about Gossip Harbor gameplay.

How do I get more energy?+
Energy is the most important resource in Gossip Harbor, and there are many ways to obtain it. Your energy regenerates naturally at a rate of +1 energy every 2 minutes, which adds up to 720 energy per day from passive regeneration alone. On top of that, you can collect a free energy pack each day from the daily login rewards. Merge events frequently reward energy as well, and the Brimwave Community feature provides additional energy at higher community levels. Connecting your game to Facebook grants access to free gifts from friends, which can include energy. The Flaming Frenzy event (unlocked at level 35) is one of the best energy sources, as it doubles your coin earnings which can be converted into progress. Rave Reviews, indicated by the purple smiley face orders, reward between 90 and 240 energy per round depending on how many you complete. Restaurant Goals milestones also grant energy rewards, and the monthly Seasons pass provides energy chests as free rewards. With proper management, a free-to-play player can earn approximately 1,000 energy per day without spending any real money. If you choose to buy energy with gems, the first purchase each day is free, and subsequent purchases cost 10 gems with the cost doubling each time after that.
What should I spend diamonds on?+
Diamonds are a precious premium currency, and spending them wisely is critical to your progress. The absolute best investment for your diamonds is Board Expansion. More board space allows you to keep more items on your board at once, which means you can merge more efficiently and waste less energy clearing space. This is the single most impactful upgrade in the game. The second priority is the Energy Cap Upgrade, which increases your maximum energy storage so you can accumulate more energy from regeneration and rewards before hitting the cap. The third priority is Generator Upgrades, which improve the output of your generators and allow you to produce higher-level items more quickly. One thing you should NEVER spend diamonds on is energy refills. Energy refills have a terrible return on investment compared to board expansions, which provide a permanent benefit. Save your diamonds exclusively for functional upgrades that improve your long-term efficiency, and avoid spending them on cosmetic decorations until you have fully expanded your board.
How do I unlock the Fishing Net?+
The Fishing Net is an important generator in the Seafood chain, and it unlocks when you reach Seafood Generator level 5. To get there, you need to upgrade your seafood generator through a series of five stages. The progression goes as follows: first you start with the Simple Hook at level 1, then upgrade to Fish's Fishing Tackle at level 2, then Bucket of Catches at level 3, then Cool Fishing Rod at level 4, and finally the Fish Net at level 5. Each upgrade requires merging generator pieces and spending energy. Once you have the Fish Net, it becomes your primary source of Raw Shrimp and Raw Fish, which are the foundational items for the entire Seafood merge chain. The Seafood chain produces some of the highest-value orders in the game, including VIP Seafood orders, so investing in this generator early is highly recommended. Keep in mind that upgrading generators requires diamonds in addition to energy, so plan your diamond spending accordingly.
What is the 5-merge rule?+
The 5-merge rule is one of the most important strategies in Gossip Harbor. Instead of merging 3 items and then merging 2 more items separately (which gives you only 1 item from the second merge), you should always wait until you have 5 items of the same level and merge all 5 at once. When you merge 5 items, the game gives you 2 items of the next level instead of just 1. This means you get a 100% bonus output compared to doing a 3-merge followed by a 2-merge. Over time, consistently applying the 5-merge rule can increase your overall efficiency by 25 to 40 percent. The key is to plan your board layout so that you always have space to accumulate 5 items before merging. This might mean leaving some board space open and being patient, but the long-term benefits are enormous. Train yourself to never merge 3 items when you could wait for 5, and you will notice a significant improvement in your progress speed.
Which orders should I prioritize?+
Order prioritization is essential for maximizing your coin income and story progression. The general priority from highest to lowest is as follows. VIP Seafood orders offer the highest rewards and should always be your top priority when they appear. Coffee orders have the best energy-to-reward ratio, meaning you spend relatively little energy to fulfill them compared to the coins and XP you receive. Bread and Sandwich orders are the most common and provide a steady income stream. Standard Seafood orders are high-value but require more energy investment. You should generally SKIP high-level orders that have a poor return on investment, such as expensive cake orders or high-level latte orders that consume enormous amounts of energy for relatively modest rewards. During active events, you should focus ONLY on event-related orders to maximize your event progress and rewards. Always check the energy cost versus the reward before accepting any order, and do not be afraid to let unfavorable orders expire.
How do energy bottles work?+
Energy bottles are one of the most important mechanics to understand in Gossip Harbor. The critical rule is that you should ALWAYS merge energy bottles to their maximum level before opening them. A level 1 energy bottle (Zap) gives only 2 energy, but as you merge them up the chain, the value increases dramatically. The full chain is as follows: Zap gives 2 energy, Blast gives 6 energy, Punch gives 15 energy, Shock gives 40 energy, and the maximum level Big Bolt gives 100 energy. As you can see, a max-level Big Bolt gives 100 energy compared to just 40 energy from a level 4 Shock, which is 2.5 times more value. If you open lower-level bottles instead of merging them up, you are throwing away a huge amount of potential energy. Always collect energy bottle drops on your board and merge them in groups of 5 (following the 5-merge rule) until you reach the Big Bolt level before tapping to collect. This single habit will dramatically increase your daily energy income.
What events are worth doing?+
Not all events in Gossip Harbor are created equal, and knowing which ones to prioritize will help you use your energy and time most effectively. The number one priority event is Flaming Frenzy, which unlocks at level 35 and doubles your coin earnings for 14 hours. This is the single best event in the game for accelerating your progress. The second priority is Card Collection events, which offer exclusive rewards that cannot be obtained anywhere else, including special decorations and rare items. The third priority is Restaurant Goals, which provide milestone rewards including 150 energy, gems, and a 5-star reward pack. The fourth priority is the monthly Seasons event, which offers a progression track with valuable free rewards. The fifth priority is merge events, which grant energy and gem shards as rewards. Lower priority events can still be worth doing if you have spare energy, but always focus on the higher-priority events first. During any active event, adjust your order strategy to focus on event-specific orders for maximum reward efficiency.
How do I get more board space?+
Board space is the most valuable resource in Gossip Harbor after energy, and the only way to get more of it is by purchasing board expansions with diamonds. Board expansion is universally considered the BEST diamond investment in the entire game. Having more board space means you can keep more items on your board simultaneously, which allows you to merge more efficiently using the 5-merge rule. It also means you waste less energy because you do not have to sell or discard items to make room for new drops. With a larger board, you can set up staging areas for different merge chains and keep your generators running without interruption. Every board expansion you purchase provides a permanent, cumulative benefit to your gameplay efficiency. You should save all of your diamonds specifically for board expansions before spending on anything else. The difference between a small board and a fully expanded board is like night and day in terms of how smoothly the game plays and how fast you can progress.
Is the game pay-to-win?+
No, Gossip Harbor is not pay-to-win. The game is very friendly to free-to-play players, and with proper strategy, F2P players can progress at a satisfying pace without ever spending real money. The key strategies for F2P success are straightforward. First, focus on the Coffee and Bread merge chains, which provide the best energy-to-reward ratios and are the most efficient for earning coins and XP. Second, always merge your energy bottles to the maximum level before opening them, as this effectively multiplies your energy income. Third, save every diamond you earn and spend them exclusively on board expansions, which is the single best investment in the game. Fourth, participate actively in events, especially Flaming Frenzy and Restaurant Goals, which provide generous free rewards. Fifth, log in every day to collect your daily free pack and other login rewards. While paying players will progress faster, the game does not have any content that is exclusively locked behind paywalls, and all story chapters and features are accessible to F2P players.
How do I progress faster?+
If you want to speed up your progress in Gossip Harbor, there are seven key strategies you should follow. First, always use the 5-merge rule to maximize your item output. Second, focus your energy on the Coffee chain first, then Bread, then Fries, as these have the best energy efficiency. Third, merge energy bottles to their maximum level before opening them to get the most energy per bottle. Fourth, prioritize story tasks and main quest objectives, as completing these unlocks new generators, merge chains, and board space. Fifth, participate in every event you can, especially Flaming Frenzy for double coins and Restaurant Goals for milestone rewards. Sixth, save your diamonds exclusively for board expansions, which provide the biggest permanent boost to your efficiency. Seventh, log in every single day to collect your free energy pack, daily rewards, and friend gifts. By consistently applying all seven of these strategies, you will progress significantly faster than the average player, even without spending real money.
What are Rave Reviews?+
Rave Reviews are special orders that appear as purple smiley face icons on your order board. They are an excellent source of energy and other rewards. Each round of Rave Reviews typically includes 6 to 12 orders for you to complete. The rewards for completing Rave Reviews are generous: each completed order gives you between 7 and 12 items ranging from levels 1 to 7, and the total energy reward for a full round ranges from 90 to 240 energy depending on how many orders you complete and their difficulty. There is also a Super Rave Reviews variant that has a 2-hour time limit and grants 60 percent extra rewards compared to the standard version. Rave Reviews are one of the best energy sources in the game, so you should always prioritize completing them when they appear. Plan your energy spending around Rave Review rounds to make sure you have enough energy to complete as many orders as possible. The items you receive as rewards can also be merged and used for other orders, creating a positive feedback loop that accelerates your progress.
How does the Generator Booster work?+
The Generator Booster is a mechanic that allows you to increase the output level of your generators at the cost of additional energy. There are three booster levels available. The x1 booster is the default setting and costs 1 energy per bubble pop, producing items at the generator's current level. The x2 booster costs 2 energy per bubble pop and skips one level, producing items one level higher than the generator's base output. The x4 booster costs 4 energy per bubble pop and can skip multiple levels, producing items several levels higher. In general, you should use the x1 booster most of the time because it gives you more bubble pops per energy spent, which means more items on your board for merging. More items on your board enables better 5-merge opportunities and more efficient overall production. Higher boosters are useful when you need specific high-level items quickly or when you want to save board space by producing fewer but higher-level items. However, they cost significantly more energy per item produced, so use them strategically rather than as your default setting.
What is Prepped Seafood?+
Prepped Seafood is a special item in the Seafood merge chain that is created by merging 5 Raw Shrimp or Raw Fish together, costing 16 energy total. Once created, Prepped Seafood can be used to produce various seafood dishes for customer orders. However, there is an important limitation: each Prepped Seafood item expires after 12 uses, meaning it can only fulfill 12 orders before it disappears. Fortunately, there is a clever trick to extend its life indefinitely using Scissors. When your Prepped Seafood has been used 11 times (one use away from expiring), use Scissors to split it into two lower-level items, then re-merge those two items back into Prepped Seafood. This resets the use counter back to zero, effectively giving you a fresh Prepped Seafood item for free. This scissors trick is one of the most important advanced techniques in the game, as it allows you to maintain your Prepped Seafood indefinitely without spending additional energy to create new ones.
How do I get free diamonds?+
There are several reliable ways to earn free diamonds in Gossip Harbor without spending real money. The daily free pack is the most consistent source and should be collected every single day. You can also watch an advertisement to open an energy chest, which sometimes contains diamonds. Merge events frequently reward gem shards that can be collected and merged into full gems. The Brimwave Community feature provides gems at specific community milestones: reaching community level 4 gives you a Level 2 Gem, and reaching level 13 gives you a Level 3 Gem. Restaurant Goals milestones include diamond rewards at various achievement tiers. The monthly Seasons pass offers diamonds among its free reward track. Connecting your game to Facebook allows you to send and receive free gifts from friends, which can include diamonds. Additionally, some story milestones and achievement unlocks grant one-time diamond bonuses. By consistently participating in all of these activities, a dedicated F2P player can accumulate a steady stream of diamonds over time, enough to afford regular board expansions and other essential upgrades.
What are Frozen Items (Ice Cubes)?+
Frozen Items, commonly called Ice Cubes, are a special mechanic in Gossip Harbor that can appear randomly when you merge items on your board. When a Frozen Item appears, it encases one of your items in ice, and you must wait for the ice to melt before you can use or merge that item again. To clear a Frozen Item, you need to merge it with another Frozen Item of the same level. If you do not merge it in time, the ice will crack and the item will turn into coins after 8 to 10 minutes. The ice timers vary by level: lower-level freezes last around 2 minutes, mid-level freezes last about 6 minutes, and higher-level freezes can last up to 15 minutes. Frozen Items are triggered by specific conditions: you must have gone at least 8 hours since your last freeze, you must have bought energy today, and your current energy must be below 51. Notably, Tea and Fries generators do not produce Frozen Items, so you can safely merge those chains without worrying about freezes. When managing Frozen Items, try to match levels quickly to free your items before they expire.
What is Flaming Frenzy?+
Flaming Frenzy is one of the best events in Gossip Harbor and unlocks when you reach level 35. It is a 14-hour limited-time event where you collect coins from completed orders to earn flames, which are the event currency. There are three tiers of rewards based on how many flames you collect. The Silver Dish tier requires 800 flames, the Gold Dish tier requires 1,800 flames, and the Ruby Dish tier requires 3,000 flames. Each tier offers progressively better rewards including energy, gems, and exclusive items. The key strategy for Flaming Frenzy is to focus exclusively on completing orders that generate the most coins during the event window. There is also a useful delay trick: if you stay on your game board after the event timer ends, you can continue collecting pending rewards and completing in-progress orders before the event officially closes. This gives you a few extra minutes to push for the next reward tier. Flaming Frenzy is considered the best event in the game because the double coin effect dramatically accelerates your overall progress.
How do Seasons work?+
Seasons is a monthly recurring event in Gossip Harbor where you complete various tasks to earn points and progress through a reward track. Each Season lasts for approximately one month and resets at the start of the new month. The tasks you can complete include earning coins (worth 200 to 1,750 points depending on the target), serving customers (worth points for serving 5 to 32 customers), spending gems (5 gems for 175 points), and performing merges (100 merges for a set point value). The reward track has 42 levels, with point requirements ranging from 0 points for Level 1 up to 7,000 points for Level 42. As you progress through the levels, you unlock various free rewards including energy chests, gems, and other useful items. The Energy Chest reward is particularly valuable as it provides a significant energy boost. Seasons are one of the most consistent sources of free rewards in the game, and you should try to complete as many tasks as possible each month to maximize your rewards. Even if you cannot reach the highest levels, the lower-tier rewards are still worth the effort.
What is the Gift Shop Owner?+
The Gift Shop Owner is a special customer character in Gossip Harbor with unique order mechanics. Orders from the Gift Shop Owner do not count toward your main 6 active order slots, which means you can fulfill their orders without blocking your regular customer orders. However, their orders DO count toward Rave Reviews progress, making them valuable for completing Rave Review rounds. A useful strategy is to keep one Gift Shop Owner order active on your board at all times as a permanent order. This gives you a free rave credit whenever you complete it, without taking up one of your precious main order slots. The Gift Shop Owner will never order items that cost A Coins, so you do not need to worry about expensive premium orders from them. The gifts they order include Books worth 10 points, Chocolates worth 10 points, Flowers worth 8 points, and Watches worth 12 points. Understanding the Gift Shop Owner's mechanics allows you to efficiently manage your order board and maximize your Rave Reviews progress simultaneously.
How do I use Scissors?+
Scissors are a versatile tool in Gossip Harbor that split a multi-level item into two lower-level items. When you use Scissors on an item, it divides that item into two pieces of the level below it. These two pieces can then be re-merged back into the original item, and this process can be repeated infinitely, which effectively allows you to store items in a compressed form and save board space. The most critical use of Scissors is with Prepped Seafood. After a Prepped Seafood item has been used 11 times (one use before it expires at 12), you should use Scissors to split it into two lower-level items, then re-merge those two items back into Prepped Seafood. This resets the use counter back to zero, giving you a fresh item without spending any additional energy. Additionally, Scissors themselves can be merged: merging 2 Scissors together gives you 1 Scissors that is usable 2 times, effectively doubling your Scissors efficiency. Always keep some Scissors on your board for this essential maintenance task.
What is the Orange Tree?+
The Orange Tree is a unique generator in Gossip Harbor because it does NOT consume energy to produce items. Instead, it only requires time. The Orange Tree needs a 3x3 empty space on your board to function, so make sure you allocate this space before placing it. Once active, the Orange Tree produces Whole Oranges and Orange Slices over time, which are used in various merge chains and recipes. Higher levels of the Orange Tree produce more items and higher-level oranges. One interesting mechanic is that making 2 Deluxe Dream Cakes will automatically generate an order for oranges, providing a reliable way to use your orange production. A recommended strategy is to stockpile oranges in a designated staging area on your board so that you always have a supply ready when needed for orders or events. Since the Orange Tree does not cost energy, it is essentially a free resource generator, and you should always keep it running. The only cost is the board space it occupies, but the free items it produces make this investment well worth it.
