FAQ

Get answers to the most common questions about Gossip Harbor gameplay.

How do I get more energy?+
Energy regenerates naturally at +1 every 2 minutes. You can also get energy from: daily login rewards, friend gifts (up to 20/day), reward ads, energy bottles (merge to max before opening!), Orange Tree (free), and events. F2P players can earn ~1000 energy per day with proper management.
What should I spend diamonds on?+
Priority order: 1) Board Expansion (BEST value), 2) Energy Cap Upgrade, 3) Generator Upgrades. NEVER spend diamonds on energy refills - they have terrible ROI. Save diamonds for functional upgrades only, not decorations.
How do I unlock the Fishing Net?+
The Fishing Net unlocks at Level 5+. Upgrade it to unlock the fish mother piece which is essential for the Seafood chain. The Fishing Net produces Shrimp and Fish for high-value seafood orders.
What is the 5-merge rule?+
Always merge 5 items instead of 3 when possible. Merging 5 gives you 2 items instead of 1 - that's a 100% bonus! This single habit can increase your efficiency by 25-40% over time. Plan your board to enable 5-merges whenever possible.
Which orders should I prioritize?+
Priority: VIP Seafood (highest rewards) > Coffee (best energy-to-reward ratio) > Bread/Sandwich (most common) > Seafood (high coins). SKIP Cake/High-level Latte orders - they're too energy-intensive with poor ROI. During events, focus ONLY on event orders.
How do energy bottles work?+
CRITICAL: Always merge energy bottles to MAX level before opening! Lv4 bottle = 40 energy, Max level = 100 energy. That's 2.5x more value! Never open low-level bottles - always merge them up first.
What events are worth doing?+
Priority: 1) Flaming Frenzy (double coins - BEST), 2) Card Collection (exclusive rewards), 3) Sam's Road Trip (milestone rewards), 4) Pinata Party (fun with decent rewards). Skip events with poor reward-to-effort ratios.
How do I get more board space?+
Board expansions can be purchased with diamonds. This is the BEST diamond investment in the game. More space = more efficient merging = less wasted energy. Save diamonds specifically for board expansions.
Is the game pay-to-win?+
No! Gossip Harbor is very F2P-friendly. With proper energy management and smart strategies, F2P players can progress at a good pace. The key is: focus on Coffee/Bread chains, merge energy bottles to max, and save diamonds for board expansions.
How do I progress faster?+
1) Use the 5-merge rule, 2) Focus on Coffee > Bread > Fries chains, 3) Merge energy bottles to max, 4) Prioritize story tasks, 5) Participate in events (especially Flaming Frenzy), 6) Save diamonds for board expansion, 7) Log in daily for rewards and friend gifts.
