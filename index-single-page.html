🚀 Getting Started

Complete Beginner's Guide

Everything you need to know to start your journey in Brimwave Island. Master these fundamentals and you'll progress faster than 90% of new players.

1

🔗 Always Keep Merging

Never let your merge board get cluttered. A full board means wasted energy drops. Merge items whenever possible to free up space.

2

📖 Focus on Story Tasks First

Story tasks give the best rewards and unlock new areas. Prioritize merging items needed for active story goals before random merges.

3

⚡ Save Your Energy

Don't waste energy on random merges. Plan ahead — check what items you need for current tasks and only merge those chains.

4

⏱️ Collect Energy Timers

Energy recharges over time (+1 every 2 minutes). Log in regularly to collect free energy before it caps out. Set reminders!

5

🏆 Join Events Early

Events offer exclusive rewards. Start them as soon as they appear so you have maximum time to complete all milestones.

6

🏠 Don't Rush Decorations

Decoration choices are reversible — you can change them anytime. Focus on story progress first, decorate later.

7

📺 Watch All Ads

Watch every available reward ad for bonus energy. Even though Gossip Harbor gives less than other games, every bit counts!

8

🎁 Claim Daily Rewards

Log in every day to claim your daily rewards. The streak bonus increases over time, so don't break your streak!

9

🧹 Clean the Board Before Logging Off

Before closing the game, merge everything possible. This ensures you start fresh next time with maximum board space.

10

💎 Save Diamonds for Board Expansion

Board expansions are the best diamond investment. More space = more efficient merging = less wasted energy.

11

⚙️ Upgrade Generators Strategically

Focus upgrades on generators that produce items for your current story tasks. Don't spread upgrades evenly across all generators.

12

🍽️ Complete High-Value Orders First

Customer orders have different coin/XP rewards. Prioritize orders with better rewards and skip low-value ones when busy.

13

👥 Add Friends for Daily Gifts

Connect with friends to send and receive energy gifts. Up to 20 gifts per day = 100-200 free energy!

14

📅 Check Event Calendar

Plan your play sessions around events. Coin Rush weekends are perfect for earning decoration money!

15

📖 Read All Dialogue

Story dialogue contains hints about upcoming tasks and hidden secrets. Don't skip through — you might miss important clues!

🎯 Chain Selection Strategy (Real Game Data)

Priority Order: Coffee > Bread > Fries — these are the most common orders with the best energy-to-reward ratio.
Low-level Coffee Orders: Complete ~5 orders below level 7, then switch to Shrimp orders for better returns.
Cake / High-level Latte: F2P players should SKIP these — they are extremely energy-intensive with poor ROI.
Pet Orders: NOT recommended during events — they consume too much energy and have long wait times.
Energy Bottles: Always merge to MAX level before opening! A max-level bottle gives 100 energy vs 40 at Lv4.
Orange Tree: Free energy source (no energy cost, auto-generates). Use Scissors to cut down high-level oranges for merging.

💡 Pro Tip: Real Game Merge Chain Strategy

1. Coffee Pot is King: The Coffee Pot produces both Coffee and Tea. Coffee has a higher drop rate — focus on Coffee orders first. 2. Basket Drop Rates: The Basket primarily drops Bread materials. Fries (Potato) drop at ~10% rate — don't waste energy farming them specifically. 3. Fishing Net Upgrade: Upgrade the Fishing Net to unlock the fish mother piece — this is essential for the Seafood chain. 4. Energy Bottle Rule: NEVER open low-level Energy Bottles! Always merge them to max first. Lv4 = 40 energy, Max = 100 energy. 5. Scissors + Orange Tree: Use Scissors to cut high-level Orange Tree items down one level for easier merging. 6. Event Strategy: During events, focus ONLY on event orders. Skip pet orders and high-level cake/latte orders to conserve energy.

🔧 Key Generator Mechanics

Mother Piece Cooldown: Each generator's "mother piece" produces a set number of items before entering cooldown. You must wait for cooldown to end before it can produce again.
Scissors Tool: Can cut high-level items down by one level. Useful when you need lower-level items for merges or tasks.
Hourglass Tool: Reduces mother piece cooldown time. Use on your most important generators when you're in a hurry.
Energy Boost (Lv26+): Unlocks at Lv26. Each generator tap gives +2 energy, but the output item level is +1 higher. Despite the higher level output, this actually saves energy overall!

💡 Generator Optimization Strategy

1. Orange Tree is your best friend: It's the ONLY auto generator (no energy cost!). Always keep it running. 2. Prioritize by task needs: Only invest energy in generators that produce items for your current story tasks. 3. Use Scissors wisely: If you have high-level items you can't merge, use Scissors to downgrade them instead of wasting energy producing low-level items. 4. Hourglass timing: Save Hourglass tools for Ocean Machine and Fishing Net cooldowns — their upgrade materials are the hardest to obtain. 5. Energy Boost math: At Lv26+, the +2 energy per tap more than compensates for getting +1 level items, since higher-level items merge into what you need faster.

Cons: Uses more energy per tap, slows Season progress (fewer merges = less season XP).

💡 Generator Booster Strategy

When to use 1x: During Season events when you want maximum merge count for season progress. When to use 2x: Balanced approach — moderate energy use with faster item progression. When to use 4x: When you need a specific high-level item urgently and have energy to spare. Trade-off Warning: Higher boosts mean fewer total items produced, which means fewer merges and slower Season progress. Choose wisely based on your current priorities!

❓ Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Gossip Harbor gameplay, progression, and troubleshooting.

How do I get more energy?+
Energy comes from multiple sources: natural regeneration (+1 every 2 minutes), watching ads (15 per ad for F2P), daily system gift (100 if you used all energy previous day), daily check-in (20), event rewards (20-500+), friend gifts, and diamond purchases (emergency only). The most reliable sources are daily logins and events. Save your diamonds for board expansions instead of energy!
What should I spend diamonds on?+
Board expansions are the #1 best investment — more space means more efficient merging. Generator upgrades are second priority, especially for generators tied to current story tasks. Avoid spending diamonds on speeding up cooldowns, decorations (use coins instead), or mystery chests (terrible odds).
Why is my merge board full?+
A full board means you need to merge items more aggressively. Always merge items to their highest level before generating new ones. Consider spending diamonds on board expansion if this happens frequently. Before logging off, clean your board completely so you start fresh next session.
How do I unlock new generators?+
Generators unlock through story progression. Each chapter typically unlocks 1-2 new generators. Focus on completing story tasks to unlock all generators. Once unlocked, generators can be upgraded with coins or diamonds for faster production.
What are the best events to focus on?+
Prioritize limited-time seasonal events (they won't return) and events with energy/diamond rewards. PvP events like Cook-Off Challenge are great if you can commit time. Weekly events like Merge Marathon have good milestone rewards. Always check the rewards before committing energy to an event.
How do relationships work?+
The story explores themes of trust, betrayal, and possible new romance. Dialogue choices affect relationships and story direction. Irreversible story choices appear mid-game (levels 16-40), so choose carefully. The story also includes LGBTQ+ elements.
My game is lagging. What should I do?+
Try these steps: 1) Close other apps to free memory, 2) Clear the game cache in settings, 3) Restart your device, 4) Check for game updates, 5) Ensure you have at least 2GB free storage. If problems persist, contact support through the game settings.
How do I transfer my progress to a new device?+
Connect your game to Facebook or your Google/Apple account in settings. On your new device, install the game and sign in to the same account. Your progress will sync automatically. Do NOT uninstall the old device until you confirm the transfer worked!
What's the best way to earn coins?+
Complete customer orders, especially high-value ones from VIP customers and party groups. Save orders for Coin Rush weekends when all coins are doubled. Merge high-level items for orders rather than selling them directly. Focus on seafood and premium chains for the best coin returns.
How often should I play each day?+
For optimal progress, log in 3-4 times per day: morning (claim daily, full energy), lunch (collect regenerated energy), evening (events, story), and before bed (clean board, tap generators). Each session can be 10-15 minutes. Consistency matters more than long sessions!
Should I merge 3 or 5 items?+
ALWAYS merge 5 items when possible! Merging 5 gives you 2 items instead of 1 — that's a 100% bonus. Merging 3 gives only 1 item. This single habit can increase your overall efficiency by 25-40%. Plan your board to enable 5-merges whenever possible.
What level should I upgrade my generators to?+
Use coins to upgrade all generators to level 5 (good value). For levels 6-10 (diamonds), only upgrade your most-used generators that match current story tasks. A level 10 generator is about 2x faster than level 1, so focus diamonds on 2-3 key generators rather than spreading evenly.
How do I unlock more merge board space?+
Board expansions unlock through story progression and can be purchased with diamonds. The first expansion costs 100 diamonds, with prices increasing for larger expansions. This is the BEST diamond investment in the game — more space means more efficient merging and less wasted energy. Prioritize board expansions over all other diamond purchases.
What's the difference between regular and VIP customers?+
VIP customers appear randomly and offer 3-5x higher rewards than regular customers. They typically request premium items like seafood and sushi. Always prioritize VIP orders when you have the items ready. You can identify VIPs by their special appearance and the VIP badge on their order.
How do romance options work in the story?+
The story explores possible new romance and LGBTQ+ elements. Dialogue choices affect relationships and story direction. Irreversible choices appear mid-game (levels 16-40). New chapters release every Friday, so the story continues to evolve.
Does the game have a monthly pass or VIP system?+
Gossip Harbor uses a Battle Pass (Season Pass) system and limited-time offers rather than a traditional VIP or monthly pass. The Season/Battle Pass lets you clear tiers for predictable, consistent rewards. Limited-time offers appear periodically with various bundles. Check the in-game shop for current offers.
How do I get more diamonds for free?+
Free diamonds come from: completing tasks, opening merge challenge boxes, using official redemption codes (follow official channels), and Season/Battle Pass tier rewards. Save diamonds for emergency energy (first 2 purchases are best value) and warehouse/storage slots (diamonds only, biggest late-game bottleneck).
What happens when my board is full?+
When your board is full, you can't tap generators or receive new items. This wastes potential resources. Solution: Always merge items before your board fills up. If full, sell low-level items to create space, then merge aggressively. Consider a board expansion if this happens frequently. A clean board is essential for efficient gameplay.
How do seasonal events work?+
Seasonal events (Halloween, Christmas, Valentine's, etc.) run for 7-14 days and offer EXCLUSIVE rewards that won't return. Always prioritize completing these events fully. They introduce special merge chains and decorations. The energy investment is high, but the exclusive items are worth it. Plan ahead and save energy items for seasonal events!
Can I change my romance choice after Chapter 9?+
No, romance choices are permanent after Chapter 9. If you want to see a different romance path, you'll need to start a new game or use the chapter select feature (unlocked after Chapter 11). This is intentional — your choices have consequences! Choose carefully based on which character's story you find most compelling.
Why am I not getting enough energy from ads?+
Gossip Harbor gives approximately 40% less energy from ads compared to similar merge games. This is by design. Adapt your strategy: focus heavily on event rewards, daily logins, and friend gifts as your primary energy sources. Watch every available ad, but don't rely on them as your main energy source. Save energy items for critical moments!
