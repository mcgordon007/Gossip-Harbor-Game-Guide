💡 Other Chains Strategy

1. Energy Bottles: ALWAYS merge to max level before opening! Lv4 = 40 energy, Max = 100 energy - that's 2.5x more value!
2. Scissors: Use on Orange Tree to cut high-level items down for easier merging.
3. Hourglass: Save for Ocean Machine and Fishing Net cooldowns - their upgrade materials are hardest to get.
4. Pet Orders: NOT recommended during events - they consume too much energy with long wait times.
5. Event Chains: Focus ONLY on event orders during events. Skip regular chains to conserve energy.
