Story Guide

Follow Quinn's journey as she returns to Brimwave Island and uncovers family secrets. Complete story chapters to unlock new areas and rewards.

📖 Story Overview

Protagonist: Quinn - A woman returning to her grandmother's restaurant
Setting: Brimwave Island - A coastal town with secrets
Goal: Restore the family restaurant and uncover the truth about the past
Chapters: 150+ story chapters with more added regularly

📚 Story Chapters

1

💡 Story Progression Tips

1. Prioritize Story Tasks: Story tasks give the best rewards and unlock new areas. Always complete them first.
2. Read All Dialogue: Story dialogue contains hints about upcoming tasks and hidden secrets.
3. Prepare Items: Some story tasks require specific items. Check requirements and prepare in advance.
4. Don't Rush: Enjoy the story! There's no time limit on story chapters.
5. Check Characters: Talk to characters regularly - they may have additional tasks or information.
