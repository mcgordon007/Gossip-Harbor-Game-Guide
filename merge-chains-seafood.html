Seafood Chain

The complete guide to all Seafood merge chains in Gossip Harbor. Learn how to progress from a Simple Hook to the mighty Big Seafood Tower, master the Scissors Trick, and optimize your energy usage for maximum profit.

📖 Introduction to Seafood Chains

The Seafood system in Gossip Harbor is one of the most complex and rewarding merge chains in the game. Unlike simpler chains such as Bread or Coffee, the Seafood system is split into three interconnected sub-chains that feed into each other. Understanding how these chains interact is essential for efficient gameplay, especially as you progress through the story and unlock higher-tier customer orders.

The Seafood Generator Chain produces raw materials that feed into the Raw Fish Chain, which in turn produces the Prepped Seafood item. Prepped Seafood is the key mother piece for the Prepped Seafood Output Chain, where the real money is made. Each level of the output chain yields increasingly valuable dishes that can be sold for coins or gems, making this chain one of the most profitable in the entire game.

What makes the Seafood chain unique is the Scissors Trick, a clever mechanic that allows you to reset the Prepped Seafood counter without spending additional energy. Combined with the Generator Booster strategy, experienced players can generate enormous amounts of high-value seafood dishes from a single Prepped Seafood. This guide will walk you through every level, every strategy, and every tip you need to master the Seafood system.

🎣 Seafood Generator Chain

The Seafood Generator Chain is the starting point for all seafood production. This chain consists of 11 levels, beginning with basic fishing equipment and culminating in the Great Fishing Cooler, which produces multiple raw materials simultaneously. Each level requires you to merge two identical items to advance, and higher levels unlock the ability to generate raw ingredients passively.

Key Generator Notes

Level 5 (Fish Net) is the critical milestone where the generator first starts producing raw materials. At this point, you gain access to Raw Shrimp, which is the foundation of the entire seafood system. Reaching Level 6 (Shellfish Net) adds Fresh Fish to the output, and Level 7 (Big Shellfish Net) increases the Fresh Fish drop rate. The Level 8 Great Fishing Cooler is the top tier, producing all three raw materials including Bucket O'Fish, which appears as bubbles on the board. Focus your merge efforts on reaching at least Level 5 as early as possible to start building your seafood pipeline.

🐟 Raw Fish Chain

The Raw Fish Chain is a 5-level merge chain that transforms basic raw ingredients into Prepped Seafood, the mother piece for the output chain. Each level requires merging two of the previous item, and the energy cost doubles with each level. This chain is where your energy investment begins to add up, so efficient management is crucial.

Energy Investment Breakdown

Creating a single Prepped Seafood from scratch requires a total of 31 energy (1+2+4+8+16). This is a significant investment, which is why the Scissors Trick (explained below) is so important. Without the trick, you would need to spend another 31 energy every time your Prepped Seafood counter runs out. With the trick, you can effectively reset the counter for free, making the entire chain far more sustainable over long play sessions.

🍽️ Prepped Seafood Output Chain

The Prepped Seafood Output Chain is where the real profit lies. With 10 levels of increasingly valuable dishes, this chain offers the highest coin and gem returns in the game. Prepped Seafood serves as the mother piece, and each use advances the counter. The mother piece effectively subsidizes your energy costs across all 10 output levels.

💡 Strategy Tips

The Scissors Trick (Essential)

The Scissors Trick is the single most important strategy for the Seafood chain. Here is how it works: Prepped Seafood has a limited number of uses (it can generate items from the output chain a set number of times before it is consumed). After 11 uses of your Prepped Seafood, instead of letting it expire or spending another 31 energy to create a new one, use Scissors on the Prepped Seafood to split it into 2 Fish for Sale items.

Once you have the two Fish for Sale items, simply merge them back together to create a fresh Prepped Seafood. This effectively resets the use counter to zero, giving you another full set of uses without spending any energy on a new Prepped Seafood. Over the course of a long play session, this trick can save you hundreds of energy points.

Scissors Trick Step-by-Step

Step 1: Use your Prepped Seafood 11 times to produce output chain items.
Step 2: When the counter is nearly depleted, use Scissors on the Prepped Seafood.
Step 3: The Prepped Seafood splits into 2 Fish for Sale.
Step 4: Merge the 2 Fish for Sale back into 1 Prepped Seafood.
Step 5: Your Prepped Seafood counter is now reset. Repeat as needed.
Important: Make sure you have Scissors available before your Prepped Seafood runs out. Keep a stock of Scissors from events or orders.

Generator Booster Strategy

The Generator Booster is another powerful tool that synergizes perfectly with the Seafood chain. The strategy involves manipulating the booster multiplier to maximize the energy value of each Prepped Seafood use.

Here is the optimal approach: First, set your Generator Booster to 1x. This causes the generator to produce a single Slice of Salmon, which costs only 1 energy (without Prepped Seafood) or 17 energy (with Prepped Seafood). This is the cheapest way to get an output item.

Next, switch your Generator Booster to 4x. At this multiplier, the generator produces 3 Whole Grilled Fish per cycle. Each Whole Grilled Fish is worth 4 energy without Prepped Seafood, so 3 of them represent 15 energy worth of items. Since you only spent the energy of one Prepped Seafood use to trigger this, the 4x booster setting gives you an excellent energy-to-output ratio.

Booster Strategy Summary

Phase 1 (1x Booster): Generate 1 Slice of Salmon for minimal energy cost.
Phase 2 (4x Booster): Generate 3 Whole Grilled Fish = 15 energy worth of items from 1 Prepped Seafood use.
Combined: This two-phase approach maximizes the number of output items you get per Prepped Seafood use, making the entire chain far more energy-efficient than running at a single booster setting.

General Energy Saving Tips

Beyond the Scissors Trick and Booster Strategy, there are several other ways to save energy when working with the Seafood chain. First, always try to have your generator at the highest possible level before starting a long production session. The Great Fishing Cooler (Level 8) produces three different raw materials, which means you spend less time waiting for specific items to drop. Second, avoid selling raw fish items directly unless you have a surplus. Raw Shrimp and Fresh Fish are always in demand for customer orders and story tasks, so keeping a buffer stock is wise. Third, time your Seafood production around energy regeneration. If you know you will be away from the game for a while, start a long merge chain before you leave so that your generator continues producing raw materials while you are offline.

📋 Customer Order Tips

Seafood customer orders are among the most lucrative in Gossip Harbor, but they can also be the most demanding in terms of energy and time. Here are some tips for handling them efficiently.

Accept orders strategically: Not all seafood orders are worth your time. Low-level orders that ask for Raw Shrimp or Fresh Fish are easy to fill but offer modest rewards. Focus your energy on orders that request higher-tier items like Shrimp Linguini, Fresh Oysters, or Lobster Dinner. These orders provide significantly more coins and experience points per energy spent.

VIP Seafood Orders: VIP orders that feature seafood items should always be your top priority. These orders typically offer 3-5x the normal reward and often include gems as a bonus. If you receive a VIP order for Boiled Whole Crab or Big Seafood Tower, drop everything else and focus on completing it. The gem rewards from VIP seafood orders can fund other aspects of your gameplay, such as expanding your board or purchasing energy refills.

Story task seafood requirements: Many story tasks require specific seafood items. Always check your active story tasks before committing your seafood production to customer orders. If a story task requires Grilled Fish for Two, for example, it is more efficient to produce that item directly rather than filling customer orders and hoping the story task completes as a side effect.

Event points: Starting from Level 3 (Whole Grilled Fish) in the output chain, each item grants event points. Higher-level items grant more points, with the Big Seafood Tower providing 8 event points per item. During active events, prioritize producing the highest-level seafood items you can to maximize your event point accumulation and climb the leaderboard faster.

⚖️ Comparison with Other Chains

The Seafood chain occupies a unique position in Gossip Harbor's economy compared to other merge chains. Here is how it stacks up against the main alternatives.

vs. Bread Chain: The Bread Chain is simpler and requires less energy per merge, making it ideal for early-game players. However, the Seafood chain far outstrips Bread in terms of coin and gem potential. A single Big Seafood Tower Hot Sale yields 2040 gems, while even the best Bread items rarely exceed a few hundred gems. The trade-off is complexity: the Seafood chain requires managing three sub-chains, a mother piece counter, and the Scissors Trick.

vs. Coffee and Tea Chain: The Coffee Chain is the most accessible chain in the game, with low energy costs and fast merge times. It is excellent for steady, reliable income. However, the Seafood chain's Hot Sale gem values are dramatically higher. Where Coffee might offer 10-50 gems on a Hot Sale, Seafood items at Level 7 and above offer 300+ gems. For players focused on gem accumulation, Seafood is the clear winner.

vs. Other Chains: Other chains in Gossip Harbor, such as Dessert or Floral, tend to fall between Bread and Seafood in terms of profitability. The Seafood chain's combination of high gem values, event point generation, and the Scissors Trick energy exploit makes it the most efficient chain for experienced players who have the board space and generator upgrades to support it.

When to Prioritize Seafood

Early Game: Focus on reaching Fish Net (Level 5) to unlock raw material production. Do not invest heavily in the output chain yet.
Mid Game: Upgrade to Great Fishing Cooler (Level 8) and start building Prepped Seafood. Learn the Scissors Trick.
Late Game: Full Seafood production with Booster Strategy. Prioritize VIP orders and Hot Sales for maximum gem income.
During Events: Produce the highest-level seafood items possible to maximize event points. The Big Seafood Tower's 8 event points per item makes it the single best item for event point farming.

Final Thoughts

The Seafood chain is the backbone of a profitable Gossip Harbor strategy. While it requires more setup and knowledge than simpler chains, the rewards are well worth the effort. Master the Scissors Trick, use the Generator Booster strategically, and always prioritize high-value orders. With these techniques, you will be generating more coins and gems than you ever thought possible. Good luck, and happy merging!
