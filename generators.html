Generators Guide

Every generator in Gossip Harbor with unlock levels, drop rates, and upgrade strategies. Master your generators to maximize efficiency.

⚙️ Generator Basics

What are Generators? Generators are items that produce merge materials when tapped.
Energy Cost: Each tap costs energy (varies by generator).
Cooldown: Generators have cooldown times between taps.
Upgrades: Upgrade generators to unlock new items and improve drop rates.
Generator Booster: Special item that doubles generator output temporarily.

💡 Generator Strategy

1. Prioritize Basket & Coffee Pot: These are your most-used generators. Upgrade them first.
2. Upgrade Fishing Net: Essential for unlocking the fish mother piece for the Seafood chain.
3. Orange Tree is FREE: No energy cost! Use Scissors to cut high-level items for easier merging.
4. Use Generator Booster: Save boosters for when you need to mass-produce items for events or story tasks.
5. Don't Neglect Cooldowns: Tap generators as soon as they're ready to maximize output over time.
