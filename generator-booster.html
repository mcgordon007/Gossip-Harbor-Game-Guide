Generator Booster Guide

Learn how to maximize the Generator Booster - a powerful item that doubles your generator output temporarily.

⚡ Generator Booster Overview

What it does: Doubles the output of all generators for a limited time.
Duration: Typically 10-30 minutes depending on booster level.
How to get: Events, daily rewards, achievements, special offers.
Best use: Mass-producing items for events or story tasks.

💡 Generator Booster Strategy

1. Save for Events: Don't waste boosters on regular gameplay. Save them for high-reward events.
2. Prepare First: Before activating, make sure all generators are ready to tap (not on cooldown).
3. Have Energy Ready: Boosters are useless without energy. Stock up before activating.
4. Clear Board Space: Make sure you have board space for all the extra items.
5. Focus on Key Generators: Prioritize Basket and Coffee Pot during boosters for maximum efficiency.
6. Combine with Energy: Use energy bottles during boosters to extend your tapping session.

🎯 When to Use Generator Booster

BEST Times:
- During Flaming Frenzy events for maximum coin rewards
- When you need specific items for story progression
- During Card Collection events to complete card sets
- When VIP seafood orders are available

AVOID Using:
- When low on energy
- When generators are on long cooldowns
- When board is full and you can't merge
- During regular gameplay (save for events!)
