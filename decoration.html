🏠 Decoration Events

Decoration

Complete guide to decoration events in Gossip Harbor, including stage breakdowns, rewards, and strategies for collecting town square decorations.

Introduction to Decoration Events

Decoration events are a recurring feature in Gossip Harbor that give players the opportunity to collect unique town square decorations and other valuable items. During these limited-time events, players can earn event points by completing orders or by earning points from other ongoing events. These points are then used to progress through multiple stages, each of which unlocks new decorations and bonus rewards.

The decorations obtained from these events are primarily used to customize the town square, also known as Park Square, which is one of the main locations on Brimwave Island. Some decoration events focus on decorating other areas, such as Quinn's Garden, giving players variety in how they personalize their game world. Beyond decorations, these events also reward players with useful items like energy, rainbow gems, joker cards, and card packs of various star ratings.

Each decoration event typically features a unique theme, drawing inspiration from different cultures, historical periods, or whimsical concepts. Past events have included Egyptian-themed excursions, marionette adventures, Easter celebrations, and mysterious lost cities. The themed nature of these events makes them one of the most visually engaging aspects of Gossip Harbor, and the decorations players collect serve as lasting mementos of their participation.

How Decoration Events Work

Decoration events follow a structured format that players will quickly become familiar with after participating in one or two events. Understanding the mechanics is key to maximizing rewards and completing every stage before the event ends.

Earning Event Points

Players earn event points primarily by completing customer orders. Each order fulfilled contributes a certain number of points toward the current decoration event. Additionally, points earned from other ongoing events can also count toward decoration event progress, which means players who are active in multiple events simultaneously can accelerate their decoration event completion. This crossover mechanic makes it highly beneficial to participate in as many events as possible during a decoration event period.

Stage-Based Progression: Decoration events are divided into stages, usually totaling eight stages. Each stage contains three decoration items or reward nodes that players can unlock by spending event points. The point costs increase as players advance through the stages, with later stages requiring significantly more points per item. For example, early stages may require 400 to 650 points per item, while the final stage can demand 1,500 to 4,200 points per item.

Stage Completion Rewards: In addition to the individual items unlocked at each node, players receive a bonus reward for completing all three items in a stage. These stage completion bonuses typically include energy refills, card packs, timer boosts, and other useful resources. The quality and quantity of these bonuses improve as players progress through the stages, with the final stages offering the most valuable rewards such as 5-star XL card packs.

Event Duration: Like most events in Gossip Harbor, decoration events run for a limited time. Players must earn enough points to progress through all stages before the event concludes. Any uncollected decorations or rewards are typically lost when the event ends, making it important to plan ahead and manage energy and resources efficiently throughout the event period.

An Egyptian Excursion - Full Stage Breakdown

An Egyptian Excursion is a decoration event that ran in February 2025. It features an ancient Egyptian theme with eight stages of decorations and rewards. This event is one of the most thoroughly documented decoration events on the Gossip Harbor Fandom Wiki, making it an excellent reference for understanding the general structure of decoration events.

Stage 1 - The Beginning

Decorations: Wadjet's Watch, Sobek's Oasis, Streets of Nile Clay
Stage Completion Reward: Energy + Event Points
Notes: The first stage serves as an introduction to the event, with relatively low point requirements. Players can unlock all three decorations quickly by completing standard customer orders. The energy reward helps fuel further order completion in subsequent stages.

Stage 2 - River and Fortifications

Decorations: Flooding Fortifications, Fearless Feluccas, Glorious Gazebo (500 pts)
Stage Completion Reward: 3-star Card Pack + 400 Event Points
Notes: Point requirements begin to increase at this stage. The 3-star Card Pack is a valuable reward that can provide useful merge items. The 400 bonus Event Points help offset the higher costs of this stage.

Stage 3 - Rest and Reverence

Decorations: Astute Attendant (400 pts), Bastet's Rest (500 pts), Lunar Lounge (650 pts)
Stage Completion Reward: 15-minute Timer + 400 Event Points
Notes: This stage introduces the 15-minute Timer reward, which is a useful boost for active play sessions. The Lunar Lounge at 650 points marks a noticeable jump in cost compared to earlier items.

Stage 4 - Gods and Guardians

Decorations: Scarab Statuettes, Anubis's Gaze, Set's Sentinels
Stage Completion Reward: 100 Energy + 500 Event Points
Notes: The 100 Energy reward at this stage is a significant boost that helps players push through the more demanding later stages. The Egyptian god-themed decorations add impressive visual elements to the town square.

Stage 5 - Temples and Treasures

Decorations: Anubis's Scales, Towering Temple, King Tut's Treasures
Stage Completion Reward: 4-star Card Pack + 600 Event Points
Notes: The 4-star Card Pack is a notable upgrade from the 3-star pack awarded in Stage 2. Players who reach this stage are well into the event and should be managing their energy carefully to maintain steady progress.

Stage 6 - Warriors and Wisdom

Decorations: Horus's Guard, Neith's Weaponry, Sophisticated Sphynx
Stage Completion Reward: 150 Energy + 800 Event Points
Notes: Energy rewards continue to scale up, with 150 Energy being a substantial refill. The 800 bonus Event Points also represent a significant increase, helping players tackle the expensive final stages.

Stage 7 - Power and Dominion

Decorations: Royal Banner, Ra's Dominion (1200 pts), Pyramid of Power
Stage Completion Reward: 5-star Card Pack + 1000 Event Points
Notes: This stage features the first decoration with a four-figure point cost, as Ra's Dominion requires 1200 points. The 5-star Card Pack is one of the most valuable rewards in the event, and the 1000 bonus Event Points are essential for the final stage.

Stage 8 - The Grand Finale

Items: Item Pick Box (1500 pts), Large Rare Pack (2400 pts), Rainbow Gem (3800 pts), 150 Energy (2600 pts), Smart Chest (2500 pts), Joker Card (4200 pts)
Stage Completion Reward: 5-star XL Card Pack + 150 Energy
Notes: The final stage is the most resource-intensive, with item costs ranging from 1500 to 4200 points. Unlike previous stages, this stage offers six items instead of three, including premium rewards like a Rainbow Gem and a Joker Card. The 5-star XL Card Pack completion reward is the highest-tier card pack available in this event. Players who complete all items in this stage will have fully decorated their town square with the Egyptian theme.

Decoration Rewards

While the primary appeal of decoration events is the collection of themed decorations for the town square or other locations, these events also offer a wide range of supplementary rewards that make them worthwhile even for players who are less interested in cosmetic customization.

Energy Rewards: Energy is one of the most common rewards in decoration events. Players receive energy at multiple stages throughout the event, with amounts increasing as they progress. In An Egyptian Excursion, for example, energy rewards ranged from basic energy refills in Stage 1 to 150 Energy in Stages 6 and 8. These energy rewards are crucial for maintaining active play during the event and can significantly reduce the need to spend premium currency on energy refills.

Card Packs: Card packs are awarded as stage completion bonuses and come in various star ratings. Lower-tier events may offer 3-star and 4-star card packs, while the later stages of major events reward players with 5-star and even 5-star XL card packs. These card packs contain random merge chain items that can be extremely useful for completing orders and progressing through the game.

Rainbow Gems: Rainbow Gems are a premium currency in Gossip Harbor, and they appear as rewards in the later stages of decoration events. In An Egyptian Excursion, a Rainbow Gem was available in Stage 8 at a cost of 3800 event points. Given the scarcity of Rainbow Gems through normal gameplay, this represents one of the most valuable rewards available from decoration events.

Joker Cards: Joker Cards are another highly sought-after reward, typically appearing in the final stage of decoration events. In An Egyptian Excursion, the Joker Card was the most expensive item in Stage 8, requiring 4200 event points. Joker Cards have special utility in the game, making them a premium reward that dedicated players will want to prioritize.

Special Avatars: Players who fully complete decoration events can earn special avatars as an additional reward. These exclusive avatars serve as a badge of achievement and are only available to players who finish all stages of the event within the given time frame.

Timer Boosts: Some stages award timer boosts, such as the 15-minute Timer in Stage 3 of An Egyptian Excursion. These timers can accelerate various in-game processes, making them useful for players who want to maximize their efficiency during active play sessions.

Town Square and Locations

The town square, also referred to as Park Square, is one of the main locations on Brimwave Island in Gossip Harbor. It serves as the primary location where decoration event items are displayed. When players complete decoration events, the decorations they unlock are placed in the town square, transforming its appearance to match the theme of the event.

Park Square is a central hub on Brimwave Island and is one of several key locations that players can visit and interact with throughout the game. The ability to customize this space through decoration events gives players a sense of ownership and personalization over their game environment.

While most decoration events decorate the town square, some events target different locations. For example, the Easter Jamboree event decorates Quinn's Garden rather than the town square. This variety keeps the decoration system fresh and allows players to customize multiple areas of the game world over time.

Known Locations on Brimwave Island

Brimwave Island features a variety of locations that players can explore and interact with. The main locations include:

Town Square (Park Square): The primary hub for decoration events and a central gathering place on the island.
Beach: A scenic coastal area that is part of the island's main locations.
Bertha: A named location on the island.
Blauveldt Manor: A manor location that plays a role in the game's story.
Quinn's Restaurant: A major location with multiple sub-areas including the kitchen, garden, pool, library, gym, music stage, bar, media room, and storage.
Buster's House: A residential location on the island.
Vance-Pierce's Boat: A boat location belonging to the Vance-Pierce character.

Tips for Completing Decoration Events

Completing all stages of a decoration event requires careful planning and efficient resource management. The following tips can help players maximize their event point earnings and ensure they collect every decoration and reward before the event concludes.

1. Participate in Multiple Events Simultaneously

Since points earned from other ongoing events can count toward decoration event progress, players should actively participate in as many events as possible during a decoration event period. This crossover effect can significantly boost event point earnings without requiring additional effort beyond what players are already doing for other events.

2. Prioritize High-Value Rewards in the Final Stage

The final stage of a decoration event typically offers the most valuable rewards, including Rainbow Gems, Joker Cards, and 5-star XL Card Packs. If you are running low on time or energy and cannot complete every item, prioritize the rewards that are most useful to your current game progress. Rainbow Gems and Joker Cards are generally the most valuable items due to their rarity and utility.

3. Manage Energy Wisely

Energy is the fuel that drives order completion, which in turn generates event points. Use energy rewards from earlier stages immediately to keep the momentum going. Avoid letting your energy bar sit at maximum capacity, as any energy earned beyond the cap is wasted. Plan your play sessions around energy refill times to ensure you are always making progress.

4. Focus on Orders During Active Play

Customer orders are the primary source of event points. During active play sessions, focus on fulfilling as many orders as possible. Prioritize orders that can be completed quickly with items you already have on your board, rather than spending excessive energy on complex merge chains that yield fewer points per energy spent.

5. Plan for the Point Cost Curve

Decoration events have a steep point cost curve, with later stages requiring exponentially more points than earlier ones. Players should not spend all their effort on early stages and assume the later stages will be equally easy. Save energy boosts and timer rewards for the final stages when point requirements are highest.

6. Don't Miss Exclusive Decorations

Decoration event items are exclusive to their respective events and cannot be obtained through any other means once the event ends. If you miss an event, you miss those decorations permanently. Make it a priority to at least unlock the decorations from each stage, even if you cannot afford all of the premium item rewards in the final stage.

7. Aim for the Special Avatar

Completing a decoration event in full rewards players with a special avatar that is exclusive to that event. These avatars cannot be obtained any other way and serve as a visible indicator of your dedication and achievement. If you are close to completing all stages, consider using saved resources to push through the final items and claim the avatar reward.
