Coffee & Tea Chains Guide

The highest priority chains in Gossip Harbor with the best energy-to-reward ratio. Coffee Pot produces both Coffee and Tea - master these chains for maximum efficiency.

☕ Coffee & Tea Chain Quick Facts

Coffee Chain: ~10 levels, highest priority orders
Tea Chain: ~8 levels, medium priority orders
Generator: Coffee Pot (produces both Coffee and Tea)
Energy Cost: 1-2 energy per merge
Priority: TOP - Best energy-to-reward ratio in the game

💡 Coffee Pot Strategy

1. Coffee is King: The Coffee Pot produces both Coffee and Tea, but Coffee has a higher drop rate. Focus on Coffee orders first.
2. Low-Level Orders: Complete ~5 Coffee orders below level 7, then switch to higher-value items or Shrimp orders for better returns.
3. Tea is a Bonus: Tea orders are less common and have a lower drop rate. Treat Tea as a bonus side product.
4. Energy Efficiency: Coffee items cost only 1-2 energy per merge - this is the most energy-efficient chain in the game.
5. Quick Turnaround: Coffee orders are quick to complete, making them ideal for grinding coins and XP.

🎯 Coffee vs Tea Priority

Coffee Priority: HIGH - Most common orders, best value per energy
Tea Priority: MEDIUM - Less common, lower drop rate from Coffee Pot
Strategy: Always prioritize Coffee orders. Only do Tea orders when you have the items ready or when Coffee orders are not available.
Energy ROI: Coffee chain gives the best return on energy investment in the entire game!
